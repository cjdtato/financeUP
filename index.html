Questions people ask

Why do I need a registration code?

Accounts are invite-only. The admin gives each person a one-time code, and it stops working after it's used.

Do I have to connect my bank account?

No. You enter your own income, expenses and loan details. Nothing is pulled from banks or e-wallets.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The tracker and this site adjust to phone, tablet and desktop screens.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Use Export CSV for a spreadsheet file, or Print / Save PDF for a report you can keep or share.

I used an older version that saved data in my browser. What now?

On your first login you can import that browser-saved data into your new account.

I forgot my password.

Ask the admin to reset it for you. You can then change it to a new password from inside the app.
